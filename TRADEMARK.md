## What You May Do

- **Use the code.** Fork it, modify it, redistribute it, build on it, and create derivative works, provided you comply with the Apache License 2.0.
- **Refer to the app by name** for factual, descriptive purposes ("compatible with Xenapse Studio", "a fork of Xenapse Studio", or "based on Xenapse").
- **State that your fork is based on Xenapse Studio**, provided you do not imply endorsement, affiliation, or approval by Biowess.
- **Use the name "Xenapse" or "Xenapse Studio"** in your fork's documentation, readme, or repository description to accurately describe what it is derived from.
- **Create your own visual identity.** Develop independent branding, logos, icons, splash screens, and promotional materials for your derivative project.

## What You May NOT Do

- **Distribute a fork under the name "Xenapse" or "Xenapse Studio"** as its product name. We ask (as a project policy, not a trademark claim) that forks choose a distinct name to avoid user confusion. Mentioning Xenapse Studio in your readme or docs to describe what your fork is based on is fine; shipping a product *called* Xenapse or Xenapse Studio is not.
- **Reuse the Xenapse Studio application icon** in another project, including forks and derivative works, without written permission.
- **Reuse the Xenapse Studio wordmark** in another project without written permission.
- **Reuse the splash screen or its artwork** in any project without written permission.
- **Imply endorsement or affiliation** by Biowess for any fork, derivative, or third-party product.
- **Present a modified or unofficial version** as the original Xenapse Studio application distributed by Biowess.

## Why This Policy Exists

Open-source software and brand asset protection serve different purposes.

The Apache License 2.0 ensures that Xenapse Studio's source code can be freely used, modified, and redistributed. This policy ensures that the distinctive visual assets of the project remain identifiable as belonging to Biowess.

Without these protections, unofficial forks could be distributed using the same icon, wordmark, and splash screen, making it difficult for users to distinguish the official application from independent derivatives.

**The code is open. The brand assets are not.**
